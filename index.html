About LAMFAM

Young hands.
Lasting change.

LAMFAM Charity is led by young people in The Gambia who believe you don't need to wait to make a difference. Today we are a community of thousands who give, volunteer and show up for families in need.

To contribute towards making living conditions better for the less privileged — through food, clean water, care for the sick, and Qurans and essentials for students.

A Gambia where no family goes hungry in Ramadan, every community has clean water, and young people lead the way in caring for one another.

  • Rooted in the Islamic tradition of sadaqah
  • Every delivery done by hand, by our volunteers
  • Progress shared openly with our community

What we do

Six ways we bring smiles home

Food, water, faith and care — delivered by young volunteers, hand to hand.

No — 01

Iftar for the fasting

During Ramadan we serve meals to those who are fasting and in need. Together, we have fed thousands.

No — 02

Ramadan food packages

Rice, onions, oil and essentials delivered straight to family doors, so households can focus on worship, not worry.

No — 03

Boreholes & water points

Clean, reliable water for whole communities — sadaqah jariyah that keeps flowing for years.

No — 04

Qurans & student essentials

Qurans, learning materials and everyday essentials for students and daaras, so nothing stands between them and their studies.

No — 05

Hospital visits

We visit patients, bring comfort and support, and remind them they are not forgotten.

No — 06

Equipment for communities

Practical equipment that helps communities learn, work and thrive long after we leave.

Current appeal

Water is life. Help us build the next borehole.

Many communities still walk long distances for water that isn't safe to drink. A single borehole changes that for an entire village — for children, for mothers, for students in the daara. It is charity that continues, insha'Allah, for generations.

Give in memory of a loved one. Water points can carry a dedication plaque, so their reward continues with every drop.
